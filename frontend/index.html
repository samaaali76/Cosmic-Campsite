A little piece of Earth, travelling between stars.

The Golden Record carries sounds and images chosen to introduce our world. It travels aboard the Voyager spacecraft; it was not left on a planet.

Each Voyager carries a 12-inch, gold-plated copper phonograph record with sounds and images from Earth.
